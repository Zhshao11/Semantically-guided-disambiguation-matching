### Introduction
OS 
d
ataset is a high-resolution dataset of co-registered optical and SAR patch-pairs. 
SAR images are collected from the Chinese C-band sensor, Gaofen-3, with Spotlight mode. Optical images are collected from the Google Earth platform (the copyright of optical images belongs to Google). Both SAR and optical 
patches
 are cropped from geocoding images, and the resolution is 1m. 
The OS dataset consists of 2673 non-overlapping patch-pairs with size of 512*512, and 10692 non-overlapping patch-pairs with size of 256*256. Both the optical and SAR patches are 8-bit images.
